+PLUS for GrapheneOS

Plus-ups for GrapheneOS.

+PLUS adds optional extras to a GrapheneOS phone, such as a phone app with call screening and on-device AI, while keeping verified boot and automatic updates.

You hold the signing key. The phone builds each official GrapheneOS update with your chosen add-ons and signs it with your own key. Without add-ons, +PLUS simply keeps GrapheneOS up to date.

The first plus-up: call screening

Our first plus-up for GrapheneOS is call screening, a much-requested feature that the project's author really wanted. It is highly customizable and runs entirely on the phone, with on-device search, transcripts and call notes. It is in the catalog now (test version): choose it in the +PLUS store on the phone.

Setting up a phone

To keep the first setup simple, the whole job of patching a GrapheneOS image with +PLUS, the re-signing and update tool, happens in your browser. It takes much longer than dedicated computer tools, but it is foolproof and automatic, and nothing is sent anywhere.

You can back up your own signing key for special recovery tasks, if you ever need it. Encrypt it with a hardware token, keep it in a password manager, or put it in a safety deposit box: how you keep it is up to you.

This is an early test version. More about the project will follow.
